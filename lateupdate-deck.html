Dashboards · the monitor limit

Our clients can't build the dashboards they need.

A dashboard can pull from at most 8 monitors today. Our customers track far more than that, so they hit the wall:

I discussed the current behavior with Abdulwase and the competitors with Yusr — how competitors handle it, what customers expect, how they use dashboards. Three ways to support more than 8 monitors:

◷Refresh on a schedule
Past 8 monitors, the dashboard shows data refreshed every 3 hours instead of re-loading every monitor each time it's opened.
▦Limit what each monitor shows
Limit how many widgets each monitor can have, so every extra monitor adds a small, predictable cost.
◧Trade time range for data
The more monitors data on a dashboard, the shorter its allowed date range — a year, then a month, then 7 days — keeping total data in check.
All three break the link between "more monitors" and "more load", so wide dashboards stay fast.
→Taking all three to Talaat in planning meeting tomorrow to choose one.
